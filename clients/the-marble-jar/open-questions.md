# Open questions for Mithun (before choosing the main campaign offer)

Asked by Quailworks, 2026-11-06. Section A blocks the campaign; B and C shape targeting and proof;
D is needed before the first email goes out.

## A. The offer (blocks everything)

1. **Which one service should outbound lead with?** Rank these, or name another:
   automation of manual processes · custom development · integrations · the new November platform.
2. **Integrations:** are they still offered? If yes, for which systems (for example NetSuite,
   QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, Salesforce, HubSpot), and may emails say
   "we build on top of your existing systems"?
3. **The new platform:** what is it called, what does it do, who is it for, how is it priced, and is
   it ready for demos or trials? Should outbound sell it now, from launch, or not yet?
4. **Deal shape:** typical project price and length, the smallest deal worth taking, fixed price or
   time-and-materials, and which currency to quote in (USD, GBP, EUR).
5. **First step for prospects:** can you deliver a free 45-minute process scan plus a clickable
   prototype within 5 working days? If not, what free first step can you offer (audit, demo, workshop)?

## B. Who to target in the US, UK and Ireland

6. Which industries and company sizes have you delivered for, and any to avoid? Is 50-500
   employees right?
7. Who signed your past deals: COO, CFO, CTO / Head of IT, operations manager, founder?
8. Do you already have English-speaking clients (US, UK, Ireland)?
9. Can you serve US clients well: time zones (Breda and Kochi), meetings, contracts, invoicing?

## C. Proof and positioning

10. Which newer clients, logos and results may we name in outreach?
11. When will the refreshed website be live (no Mendix, newer clients, new platform)? Emails link to
    themarblejar.io from email 2 on, so the timing decides whether we launch before or after it.

    booking link that shows US and UK time zones.
