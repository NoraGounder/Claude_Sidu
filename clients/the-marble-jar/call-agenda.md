# 30-minute call with Mithun: agenda

Goal: leave the call with everything we need to start finding new clients. The full list of
questions is in `open-questions.md`; this call covers only what blocks the launch.

1. **The offer (10 min)**
   - Which one service should we lead with: automation of manual work, custom development,
     integrations, or the new platform?
   - Are integrations still offered? With which systems?
   - The new platform: sell it now, from launch, or not yet?
   - What free first step can you deliver for a prospect (for example a short process scan and a
     clickable prototype within 5 working days)?
2. **Money (5 min)**
   - Typical project price and length, and the smallest project worth taking.
3. **Your best clients (10 min)**
   - Your 10 best clients: name, website, why they bought, and who signed.
   - Who do you compete with most, and why do you win or lose?
4. **Who never to email (3 min)**
   - Current clients, open deals and partners. A list after the call is fine.
5. **Quick yes or no (2 min)**
   - Footer address: The Marble Jar BV, Verlengde Poolseweg 16, 4818 CL Breda.
   - Start small: about 115 Dutch companies that are hiring admin staff or fit best, about 40 emails
     a day, sent in your name.

**After the call, please send:** the best-clients list, the do-not-contact list, your booking link,
and one email you wrote that got a good reply.
