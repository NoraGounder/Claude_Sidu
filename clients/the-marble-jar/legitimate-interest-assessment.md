# Legitimate interest assessment: The Marble Jar outbound (2026-11-17)

Required by article 3.1 of the data processing agreement (audit H9). The Marble Jar is the
controller; Quailworks prepared this on its behalf. GDPR article 6(1)(f), three tests.

**Separate from this:** the Dutch and Belgian e-privacy rules on commercial email (Telecommunicatiewet
art. 11.7; Belgian Code of Economic Law art. XII.13) ask for prior consent for email to a named
person's work address. That is not a GDPR question and this assessment does not answer it. Sidarth
accepted that risk for the first batch (decisions.md D18, D20).

## 1. Purpose: is there a legitimate interest?

Yes. The Marble Jar wants to find companies that could use its automation and custom software
services and offer them a 30-minute conversation. Direct marketing is named as a possible legitimate
interest in recital 47 GDPR. The interest is real and current: The Marble Jar needs new clients.

## 2. Necessity: is the processing needed for it?

- Only companies with a concrete reason to care are contacted: in our sectors, 50-500 employees, and
  with an open vacancy for administrative work that The Marble Jar's service can reduce.
- Per company, at most two people whose job is the work in question (operations, finance, the owner
  of a smaller company); one at a time.
- Only business data is used: name, job title, company, work email, LinkedIn page, source. Nothing
  private, no phone numbers, no special categories, no tracking pixels or click tracking.
- A less intrusive way that works as well is not available: the companies do not know The Marble Jar,
  and the people who decide on this work are not reachable through a general address.

## 3. Balancing: do the person's interests override it?

- **Expectations:** the data is about the person's job, used to contact them about their job. Senior
  operations and finance people at companies of this size expect occasional business offers.
- **Impact:** at most four short, plain emails over two weeks, then nothing. No profiling beyond the
  company's public vacancy and the person's job title.
- **Safeguards:** every email states who sends it, where the details came from, and how to stop;
  a "stop" or unsubscribe is honoured at once and suppressed for good; contacts who did not reply are
  deleted on the agreed schedule; data stays out of the code repository and is backed up encrypted.
- **Vulnerable people:** none; business roles only.

**Result:** the legitimate interest holds for business contacts in the Netherlands, Belgium, the UK,
Ireland and the US under these safeguards. Review when the volume, the data sources or the type of
people contacted change, or after any complaint.
