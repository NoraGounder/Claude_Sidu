# Quailworks: offer and qualification (Layer 0)

Status: **decided by Quailworks (2026-11-10)**, see `decisions.md`. Results change it, not questions.

## The offer we lead with

A done-for-you outbound system for companies that sell to manufacturers and industrial teams.
Quailworks builds and runs it: it finds the companies that show a reason to buy right now, finds the
right person there, writes from facts on that company's own website (with the source for each), and
books the first call. The client's team takes the calls and keeps the system, built on their own
stack and documented. Source: quailworks.xyz ("Outbound Automation", "Hand off: you own the system,
completely", "We build on your existing stack").

The next step we offer is a 30-minute call. Email 3 offers a lighter alternative: 10 companies in the
prospect's market that show one of these signals now, each with its source (decisions.md Q5).

## Never in outreach

- Results, meeting counts, response rates, guarantees, timelines or prices (Q3, Q6).
- The Marble Jar or any other client by name (Q4).
- Workflow automation or AI agents in email 1 (Q1); they come up in replies and calls.

## Pricing (calls only, never in email)

First engagement under $3,000 (the user's answer, 2026-11-10); project-based or retained, as on
quailworks.xyz. Revisit after the first three calls (Q6).

## What counts as a qualified meeting

All four must be true:

1. The company matches the ICP in `icp.yaml`: US, 11-200 employees, sells to manufacturers or
   industrial teams, not excluded.
2. The person decides on, or strongly influences, how the company finds new customers.
3. They replied with interest in the offer, not just curiosity.
4. The meeting took place.

After each call the founder records one word: qualified, not a fit, or follow up.

## Disqualify

- Fewer than 11 or more than 200 employees (Q10)
- Lead-generation, marketing, sales and appointment-setting agencies (they sell what we sell)
- Staffing and recruiting firms, associations, non-profits, schools and universities, public bodies
- Companies outside the United States (Q8)
- The Marble Jar, and automation or software studios in the Netherlands and Belgium while it is a
  client (Q14, Q15); current clients and partners in `private/exclusions.csv`
