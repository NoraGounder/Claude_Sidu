# Quailworks: offer and qualification (Layer 0)

Status: **decided by Quailworks (2026-11-10)**, see `decisions.md`. Results change it, not questions.

## The offer we lead with

Signal-based outbound, built as a system, for companies that sell to hardware engineering teams in
aerospace, space, automotive and advanced manufacturing. Quailworks finds the accounts that show a
reason to buy right now, finds the right person, writes from facts on that company's own website (with
the source for each), and books the first call. Everything runs inside the client's own tools and they
own all of it. Source: quailworks.co ("Signal-based outbound: find accounts that are ready to buy, and
reach them with a reason to reply"; "We were an engineering firm before we were a GTM firm"; "You own
everything").

The founder spent over a decade as a mechanical design engineer on aerospace and high-performance
automotive programs, choosing and managing suppliers: the buyer these companies are trying to reach.

The next step we offer is the one on quailworks.co: a 30-minute working session and a written note on
the three things we would fix first, whether or not we work together. Email 3 offers a lighter
alternative: 10 companies in their market that show a signal now, each with its source.

## Never in outreach

- Results, meeting counts, response rates, guarantees, prices or build times (Q3, Q6).
- Client names, or the founder's past employers and programs (Q2, Q4).
- Inbound routing, CRM data or AI agents in email 1 (Q1); they come up in replies and calls.

## Pricing (calls only, never in email)

The path on quailworks.co: a GTM audit at a fixed fee (the first engagement, under $3,000, the user's
answer of 2026-11-10), then a build sprint quoted after the audit, then an embedded team or a handover.
Revisit after the first three calls (Q6).

## What counts as a qualified meeting

All four must be true:

1. The company matches the ICP in `icp.yaml`: US, 11-500 employees, sells to engineering teams in
   aerospace, space, automotive or advanced manufacturing, not excluded.
2. The person decides on, or strongly influences, how the company finds new customers.
3. They replied with interest in the offer, not just curiosity.
4. The meeting took place.

After each call the founder records one word: qualified, not a fit, or follow up.

## Disqualify

- Fewer than 11 or more than 500 employees (Q10)
- Lead-generation, marketing, sales and appointment-setting agencies (they sell what we sell)
- Staffing and recruiting firms, associations, non-profits, schools and universities, public bodies
- Companies outside the United States for now (Q8)
- The Marble Jar, and automation or software studios in the Netherlands and Belgium while it is a
  client (Q14, Q15); current clients and partners in `private/exclusions.csv`
