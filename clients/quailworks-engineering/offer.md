# Quailworks Engineering: the Grand Slam Offer (v2, 2026-11-25)

Built with Alex Hormozi's $100M Offers method (Value Equation, problems → solutions, trim and stack, scarcity,
urgency, bonuses, guarantee, MAGIC naming; skills in `.claude/skills/hormozi-offer`, `bonus-stack`,
`audit-offer`, `objection-destroyer`). Decided by Quailworks (CLAUDE.md rule 8), see `decisions.md` E15-E19;
the user can veto any number before the first send. v1 (2026-11-19) sold "design capacity on a day rate":
a commodity buyers compare on price with agency contractors. v2 sells an outcome with the risk on us.

## 1. Market and avatar (the gate)

| Test | Read | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Massive pain | great | A build, test or show date that will not move, and a design role open for months |
| Purchasing power | great | Funded hardware start-ups, motorsport budgets, OEM-backed programmes |
| Easy to target | great | They advertise the pain: an open mechanical design job (BlitzAPI, job boards) |
| Growing | normal to great | EV, space, eVTOL, robotics growing; motorsport steady |

**Avatar:** the Head of Engineering, Chief Engineer or Technical Director of a UK or US hardware company with
11-500 people (motorsport, performance and low-volume vehicles, space and aerospace, robotics), who has a
dated build and an open design role, and whose own engineers are already releasing parts late.

**Positioning (category of one):** not "a contract designer" (compared on day rate) but *the team that takes a
part off your list and hands it back supplier-ready by a fixed date, at a fixed price, or you don't pay.*

## 2. Value Equation

Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)

| Driver | v1 (day rate) | v2 (this offer) |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome | "more capacity" | **Your parts released on time for the build, without the hiring delay** |
| Perceived likelihood | a CV | Programme record, a free drawing check on *their* drawing, a written scope, the Supplier-Ready Guarantee |
| Time delay | "from next week", open-ended | Starts on a fixed date; **first part supplier-ready in 10 working days** |
| Effort & sacrifice | they onboard, manage and check a contractor | We learn their standard ourselves (Standards Pack), one 20-minute review a week, every drawing pre-checked, handover pack at the end |

The bottom of the equation is where we win: fast, and nearly no work for their team.

## 4. The money model (attraction → core → continuity)

| Step | Offer | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead magnet (cold email) | **The 40-Point Release Check**: the checklist we run on every drawing before it reaches a supplier (`assets/40-point-release-check.md`) | free, no call | Shows how we work, reveals the release backlog |
| Attraction (after a reply) | **Free Drawing Check**: one of their released drawings marked up within 48 hours, under NDA | free | Proof on their own work: perceived likelihood |
| **Core** | **The First-Part Sprint**: one part or sub-assembly, from spec to a supplier-ready released drawing, in their CAD and to their standard, in 10 working days | fixed: **£4,500 / $6,000** (part or simple assembly, up to ~5 parts); **£8,500 / $11,500** (sub-assembly, up to ~15 parts); larger quoted | The first purchase: small, fast, risk-free |
| Continuity | **Embedded Design Engineer**: a named design engineer on their programme, in their systems | **£600 / $850 a day**, invoiced monthly, month to month, one week's notice | Recurring revenue; the Sprint fee is credited in full if they continue within 30 days |
| Downsell (not ready to outsource design) | **Release Check service**: we check and mark up their team's drawings before release | per drawing set, quoted | Keeps the relationship |

Prices are for calls and proposals only, never in email (E8). The day rate is above agency contractor rates
on purpose: we sell a dated outcome and carry the risk, so we are not compared hour for hour.

## 6. Guarantee: the Supplier-Ready Guarantee (conditional, with teeth)

> **If your part isn't released as a supplier-ready drawing, to your standard, by the date in the written scope,
> you don't pay for the Sprint.** And if your supplier finds a dimensioning or tolerancing error of ours on any
> drawing we release, we fix it within two working days, unbilled.

Conditions (written into the scope): inputs on day one (spec, interfaces, CAD access or standard), feedback on
each review within two working days, and the date moves day for day for any delay on the client's side. ROI
check (Hormozi's guarantee math): one claimed Sprint costs about 7.5 days of our time; a guarantee that lifts
first purchases by even 25% pays for several claims. Would change: two claims in the first ten Sprints.

## 7. Scarcity and urgency (both real)

- **Scarcity:** two First-Part Sprints start each month, so each gets a lead engineer's full attention. That is
  our real capacity today; it rises only when vetted engineers join (E13).
- **Urgency:** Sprints start on the 1st and the 15th; a slot closes five working days before its start date.
  Motorsport and pre-season builds get a seasonal name in autumn and winter (below).

## 9. What a qualified meeting is

All four: the company matches `icp.yaml`; the person decides on or strongly influences bringing in outside
design work (engineering lead, CTO or founder); they replied with interest in a Sprint, the Drawing Check or
embedded help; the meeting took place. After each call the founder records one word: qualified, not a fit,
follow up.

## 10. Never in outreach (E7, E19)

- Prices, day rates, results we have not had, or headcount ("our engineers", "team of"): we speak as the company
  ("we") and never imply people we do not have.
- Employer, client or programme names (programme types only).
- The word "guarantee" in a cold email: email 2 states the promise in plain words instead.
- Email to consent-first countries (Germany, Poland, Denmark and similar).

## 11. Disqualify

Fewer than 11 or more than 500 employees; engineering consultancies, design agencies, engineering staffing and
recruiters; companies without their own hardware; US defence and ITAR-controlled work; companies outside the UK
and the US for email; current clients and former employers (`private/exclusions.csv`, the warm list).
