# Quailworks Engineering: offer and qualification (Layer 0)

Status: **offer v1, decided by Quailworks (2026-11-19)**, see `decisions.md` E3-E8. Results change it,
not questions.

## Who it is for

UK and US companies with 11-500 employees that design and build their own physical products and need
more mechanical design capacity than their team has: motorsport and performance-vehicle makers, EV and
low-volume vehicle companies, commercial space, aerospace and eVTOL companies, and robotics and machine
builders (E4, E5). The moment to write: they are hiring a mechanical design engineer. Hiring takes months;
a contract designer can start next week.

## The offer: design capacity on demand

**A senior mechanical design engineer working on their programme from next week, from concept to
released, toleranced drawings, without a recruitment process.** Two ways to buy it:

1. **A named design engineer on a day rate**, working in the client's CAD and PLM (their seats or remote
   access), to their drawing standard, reporting to their engineering lead. The usual way companies already
   buy contract designers, so it needs no new process.
2. **A fixed-price design package** with a written scope: for example one suspension upright, a housing or a
   jig, from concept through design for manufacture to drawing release. Priced after a short scoping call.

What is in it, either way:

- Concept, layout and packaging; detailed 3D design and assemblies in CATIA V5/3DEXPERIENCE, Siemens NX or Creo.
- 2D drawings with GD&T (ISO and BS 8888), composite ply tables, BOMs and DMU upkeep.
- Weight and stiffness work with the client's analysis team (FEA-led changes, topology optimisation).
- Design for manufacture with their suppliers; jigs, fixtures and test rigs.
- **Design authority stays with the client.** Every drawing is self-checked against their standard before release.

## The free first step (E6)

Email 1 offers it, no call needed: **a one-page profile of the engineer who would do the work**, with
the kinds of parts designed and the CAD systems used. It answers the buyer's first question (can this
person do our work?) without a meeting. The call is offered when the profile is sent.

## Never in outreach (E7)

- Employer, client or programme names. Programme *types* only ("hypercar suspension", "a championship-
  winning touring car", "launch-vehicle turbopump parts"); names are in the profile and on LinkedIn.
- Rates, prices, team size or "our engineers" (until there is a bench), delivery times other than "next week",
  and any promise of results.
- Work in Germany, Poland or other consent-first countries by email (E4).

## Pricing (calls and proposals only, never in email; E8)

- **Day rate:** quoted per engagement, in the range clients already pay senior contract design engineers in
  that market. Floor: what the founder earned as a contractor.
- **Fixed-price package:** scoped estimate of days × day rate + 15% for the risk taken on; one revision
  round included.
- **Payment:** monthly in arrears for day-rate work; 30/40/30 for packages (start, design review, release).

## What counts as a qualified meeting

All four must be true:

1. The company matches `icp.yaml` (designs its own hardware, 11-500 employees, UK or US).
2. The person decides on, or strongly influences, bringing in outside design capacity (an engineering lead,
   CTO or founder).
3. They replied with interest in the work, not just curiosity about the profile.
4. The meeting took place (a no-show is rebooked and does not count).

After each call the founder records one word: qualified, not a fit, or follow up.

## Disqualify

- Fewer than 11 or more than 500 employees (large OEMs buy through preferred-supplier lists; E5)
- Engineering consultancies, design agencies, engineering staffing and recruiters (they sell the same thing)
- Companies without their own physical product: software-only, distributors, dealerships, repair shops
- Programmes the founder cannot legally work on from Poland: US defence and ITAR-controlled work (E5)
- Companies outside the UK and the US for email (E4); current clients and former employers in `private/exclusions.csv`
