How it works

How a signal
becomes a meeting.

Five steps, each with a written spec, tests on real data and an alert when something breaks. The same engine runs our own outbound.

01

Detect

Open sales and engineering roles, trade shows, funding and growth, read from job boards and company websites across your market.

Runs onJob postings · company sites
02

Check

A keyword on a careers page is not a hiring signal. Every match is checked: in our own data, 40% were not open roles.

Runs onTypeSafe Jev · written rules
03

Find

The people who decide, their work emails, and a second check on every address before anything is sent.

Runs onFullEnrich · MillionVerifier
04

Write

Templates written by a person, filled only with facts the engine can source. A fact check runs on every draft.

Runs onSmartlead · your domains
05

Learn

Which signal, opening and segment produce replies, meetings and revenue. What does not work gets cut.

Runs onHubSpot · weekly report

Replies, meetings and won deals feed back into scoring, so the engine gets sharper every week.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Anything else? Write to hello@quailworks.co.

Who is this for?

Companies with 11 to 1,000 employees that sell products worth $20,000 or more to engineering, manufacturing and plant teams: robotics and automation, manufacturing and industrial software, industrial AI, machine vision. It works best when you are hiring for sales, because that is budget already set aside for this job.

What is a buying signal?

A public, checkable reason to write to a company now: an open role, a trade show, a funding round, fast growth, a new certification. We read them from job boards and from each company's own website, check every one, and keep the source.

Do you run it, or hand it over?

We build it and run it for the first 90 days. After that you choose: we keep running it month to month, or your team takes it over with training and a runbook.

Who owns what you build?

You do: the sending domains, the data, the playbook and the documentation. If we stopped working together tomorrow, the engine would keep running.

Is cold email to engineers allowed?

We write only to business addresses of people whose role fits the offer, in the United States and the United Kingdom. Every first email says where we found the address, how to opt out and who we are. Opt-outs are honoured for good, and data we do not use is deleted on a schedule.

What does it cost?

The Signal Sample is free. The Signal Engine is a monthly fee for the 90 days with all tools included, quoted after a first conversation. There is no long contract: after the first term it runs month to month.

How quickly will something happen?

You have the free list within two working days. In an engagement, new sending domains need about three weeks to warm up; in that time we re-contact your lost deals and old leads. Cold sending starts around week four.

Do you have case studies?

Not yet, and we would rather say so. Quailworks is new: its first client campaigns start in late 2026, and the numbers on this page are from our own engine. That is why the first three clients get founding terms, and why the first step is a free list you can judge for yourself.
