The gap

Your market sends buying signals every day. Most revenue teams act on a fraction of them, days too late.

  1. 01

    Research done by hand

    Sellers spend hours building lists and researching accounts, one browser tab at a time. None of that work compounds.

  2. 02

    Signals that never trigger anything

    Job changes, funding rounds, hiring sprees and pricing-page visits all show intent, but no system turns them into action.

  3. 03

    A CRM nobody trusts

    Duplicates, stale fields and missing routing rules. Leads go cold, and forecasts become guesswork.

How a signal
becomes pipeline.

Every system we ship follows the same architecture. Each stage has a written spec, an owner, and an alert for when something breaks.

01

Detect

Hiring, funding, job changes, stack shifts, website visits and product usage, tracked continuously across your market.

Runs onClay · scrapers · webhooks
02

Enrich

Waterfall enrichment across several providers, so every email and phone number is verified before anything is sent.

Runs onClay · data providers
03

Score

Fit, intent and timing, scored against your own closed-won deals rather than a generic ICP template.

Runs onCRM history · LLMs
04

Act

Each signal fires the play that fits: a personal email, a LinkedIn touch, an ad audience or an alert to the right owner.

Learn

Replies, meetings and revenue sync back to the CRM. Every two weeks, plays that aren't working get cut.

Runs onSalesforce · HubSpot · BI

Outcomes feed back into scoring, so the engine gets more accurate every cycle.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Anything else? Write to hello@quailworks.co.

What is GTM engineering?

It's engineering applied to go-to-market: the data pipelines, automations and AI agents that find, research, prioritise and reach the right accounts, and keep your CRM accurate. Your sellers spend their time selling instead of preparing to sell.

Do we need to use Clay?

No. Clay is often the right core for enrichment and orchestration, but we're tool-agnostic. We build on whatever fits, including n8n, custom code, your data warehouse and your CRM's native automation, starting with the tools you already license.

Do you run campaigns, or only build systems?

Both. Some clients want a system built and handed to their own team. Others retain us to operate and improve it every month. The audit is where we work out which model fits.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything lives in your accounts: CRM, workspaces, sending domains, data, prompts and documentation. If we stopped working together tomorrow, the system would keep running.

Can you work with our security and procurement teams?

Yes. We complete security questionnaires and vendor onboarding, sign NDAs and DPAs, and work inside your environment under your access controls. Tell us your requirements early and we'll build to them.

How is an engagement priced?

Audits are a fixed fee. Build sprints are fixed-scope and quoted after the audit, so you know the cost before work starts. Embedded engineering is a monthly retainer. You'll have a written proposal within a few days of our first call.

How quickly will something be live?

Build sprints usually run four to six weeks from blueprint sign-off, with the first plays in market inside that window. Data access and approvals are the usual pacing items, so we plan around them from day one.
