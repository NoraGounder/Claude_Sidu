Privacy notice.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This notice explains how Quailworks ("we", "us") handles personal information collected through quailworks.co and when we contact businesses about our services. We collect as little as we can, use it only to respond to you, and never sell it.

Who we are

The controller of your personal information is Quailworks sp. z o.o., ul. Mikołaja Reja 34, 26-225 Gowarczów, Poland (KRS 0001108506, NIP 6582000945). You can reach us at hello@quailworks.co.

If we contacted you by email

What we hold. Your name, job title, employer, business email address, country and the address of your public professional profile. Nothing about your private life.

Where it came from. Public business sources: your employer's website, public professional profiles on LinkedIn, and FullEnrich, a business-contact data provider. The first email we send you says so.

Why, and on what basis. To offer our services to the company you work for, because your role suggests they are relevant to you. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in business-to-business marketing (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We write only to business addresses.

How to stop it. Reply "stop" to any email, or write to hello@quailworks.co. We will not contact you again, and we keep only your address on a do-not-contact list so that the opt-out keeps working.

How long we keep it. If we found your details but never wrote to you, we delete them after 90 days. If we wrote and you did not reply, we delete them 12 months after our first email.

Who else sees it. Our service providers, only to provide their services: Smartlead (sending), Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 (mailboxes), FullEnrich and MillionVerifier (finding and checking addresses) and HubSpot (our customer records). Some are based outside the European Union; for those we rely on the safeguards the GDPR provides for such transfers.

What we collect through this website

  • Information you send us. When you use the contact form or email us, we receive your name, work email, company, role and anything you choose to include in your message.
  • Basic, cookie-free analytics. We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures page views without cookies, fingerprinting or tracking you across sites.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider processes standard technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to your enquiry, arrange a consultation and, if we work together, manage our business relationship. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.

Who processes it

We rely on a small number of service providers: Cloudflare (website hosting, security, analytics and delivery of contact-form submissions to our inbox) and Google Workspace (our email). They process data on our behalf and only to provide their services.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

Retention

We keep enquiry correspondence for up to 24 months, unless it leads to an ongoing business relationship or you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its processing and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@quailworks.co. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Client data

Data we access while delivering client work is governed by the agreement with that client, including any data processing agreement, and is not covered by this notice.
