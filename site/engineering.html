FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Anything else? Write to hello@quailworks.co.

Who is this for?

Teams that design their own hardware and need more design hands than they have: motorsport teams and race-car constructors, performance, EV and low-volume vehicle makers, space, aerospace and eVTOL companies, and robotics and machine builders. Usually 10 to 500 people, often while a design role is being hired.

Which CAD and standards?

CATIA V5 and 3DEXPERIENCE, Siemens NX and Creo, with your PLM. Drawings with GD&T to ISO or BS 8888, or your own company standard.

Remote or on site?

Remote by default, working in your systems through your own seats or remote access, in the Central European time zone. On-site weeks for builds, integration or testing can be arranged.

How do you handle IP and confidentiality?

An NDA is signed before any data is shared, and the contract assigns all IP in the work to you. Your data stays in your systems.

Can you work on export-controlled programmes?

Quailworks is based in the EU. UK, European and commercial programmes are fine. US ITAR-controlled work is not something we take on.

What does it cost?

The checklist and the Drawing Check are free. A First-Part Sprint has a fixed price, set by the part's size and agreed in writing before we start. Embedded work is invoiced monthly in arrears at a rate agreed up front, and a Sprint fee is credited in full if you continue within 30 days.

How does the Supplier-Ready Guarantee work?

The scope names the part, the deliverables and the date. If the part isn't released as a supplier-ready drawing to your standard by that date, you don't pay for the Sprint. Two conditions: we need your inputs on day one, and your feedback on each review within two working days; any delay on your side moves the date by the same amount. And if your supplier finds a dimensioning or tolerancing error of ours later, we fix it within two working days, unbilled.

Is this the same Quailworks as the outbound firm?

Yes. Quailworks sp. z o.o. runs both: outbound for companies that sell to engineers, and this design service. Both come from the same engineering background.
