Weight- and packaging-critical parts are the speciality: chassis, suspension, structures, housings, composites, and the jigs and rigs that build and test them.

01 — Design

Concept to detailed 3D

Layouts that respect the hardpoints, the packaging and the weight target.

  • Concept, layout and packaging studies
  • Detailed parts and assemblies in CATIA V5 or 3DEXPERIENCE, Siemens NX or Creo

02 — Release

Drawings your suppliers can make

Fully toleranced, checked against your standard before they go out.

  • 2D drawings with GD&T to ISO or BS 8888
  • Ply tables and layup documents for composite parts
  • Design for manufacture with your suppliers
  • Self-checked before every release
  • Released through your PLM and change process
03 — Build

The tooling that gets the first parts built and measured.

  • Assembly and set-up jigs
  • Test rig brackets and fixtures

  • Support for prototype builds and testing
  • Supplier liaison through to delivery

How we work

A named lead engineer on every job. Your standard from the first part.

Every engagement has one lead engineer who answers for the work, from scope to release. When a programme needs more hands or a specialist in analysis, composites or drafting, we add vetted engineers under the same NDA, standards and checks, so the work reads as one team's.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Anything else? Write to hello@quailworks.co.

Who is this for?

Teams that design their own hardware and need more design hands than they have: motorsport teams and race-car constructors, performance, EV and low-volume vehicle makers, space, aerospace and eVTOL companies, and robotics and machine builders. Usually 10 to 500 people, often while a design role is being hired.

Which CAD and standards?

CATIA V5 and 3DEXPERIENCE, Siemens NX and Creo, with your PLM. Drawings with GD&T to ISO or BS 8888, or your own company standard.

Remote or on site?

Remote by default, working in your systems through your own seats or remote access, in the Central European time zone. On-site weeks for builds, integration or testing can be arranged.

How do you handle IP and confidentiality?

An NDA is signed before any data is shared, and the contract assigns all IP in the work to you. Your data stays in your systems.

Can you work on export-controlled programmes?

Quailworks is based in the EU. UK, European and commercial programmes are fine. US ITAR-controlled work is not something we take on.

What does it cost?

The profile is free. Day-rate work is invoiced monthly in arrears at a rate agreed up front. A design package has a fixed price, quoted after a short call about the scope.

Is this the same Quailworks as the outbound firm?

Yes. Quailworks sp. z o.o. runs both: outbound for companies that sell to engineers, and this design service. Both come from the same engineering background.
