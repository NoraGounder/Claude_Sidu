The problem

Most go-to-market still runs on duct tape.

The data to find your next customer already exists. What's missing is the system that connects it — so the right account gets the right message at the right moment, without a rep spending their morning in spreadsheets.

01

Lists built by hand

Reps research accounts one tab at a time. Every list is a one-off, and nothing compounds.

02

Signals nobody acts on

Job changes, funding rounds, hiring sprees, pricing-page visits. The intent is there — nothing triggers.

03

A CRM nobody trusts

Duplicates, stale fields and no routing rules. Leads go cold and reporting turns into guesswork.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is GTM engineering?+

It's engineering applied to go-to-market: the data pipelines, automations and AI agents that find, research, prioritise and reach the right accounts, and keep your CRM clean, so your reps spend their time selling.

Do we need to use Clay?+

No. Clay is often the right core for enrichment and orchestration, but we're tool-agnostic. We build on whatever fits, including n8n, custom code and your CRM's own automation, and we start from the tools you already pay for.

Do you run campaigns, or just build systems?+

Both. Some clients want a system built and handed over to their team. Others want us to keep operating and improving it each month. The audit is where we work out which fits.

Who owns what you build?+

You do. Everything lives in your accounts: CRM, Clay workspace, sending domains, data, prompts and documentation. If we stop working together tomorrow, the system keeps running.

Who is this for?+

B2B teams with a defined ICP whose growth is held back by manual prospecting, missed buying signals or messy CRM data, from founder-led sales through to scaling sales teams.

How quickly will something be live?+

Our build sprints run four to six weeks from blueprint sign-off, and aim to get the first plays in market within that window.
